EMBER MAZE
A Flame's Journey Through Darkness
How to Play
  1. Help Ember find the lamp.
  2. To win complete all levels in order.
  3. Ember is allergic to water, so avoid contact at all times.
  4. Pay close attention to the audio; the audio gets louder as you approach the dripping water.
  5. Use the Arrow keys to navigate through the maze, UP, RIGHT, and LEFT.
  6. Click the three lines at the top right to access the menu.
